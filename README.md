# ✨ Vibely — Emotional Media, Not Social Media 💗

Vibely is a pastel-soaked, soft-blur, mood-centered iOS app for Gen Z expression.  
You don’t post for likes. You post for **vibes**.

---

## What You Do

- 🌈 Pick your **Mood of the Week** — “main character,” “burnout,” “slay era,” etc.
- 🎶 Add your **Song of the Week** — Apple Music / Spotify integration (soon!)
- 📷 Post to your **Vibe Board** — aesthetic pics, poems, memes, quotes
- 👀 Receive or send a **Secret Crush**
- 🫶 Set your **Manifest Goals** — affirmations, mantras, moodboards
- 📚 Share your current obsessions — books, shows, events

---

## Aesthetic

- Pastel gradients  
- Soft-blur cards and inputs  
- Tap-to-reveal secrets  
- Fluid swipe navigation  
- Inspired by Pinterest x Tumblr x BeReal x Notion

---

## Core Philosophy

> It’s not social media.  
> It’s **emotional media**.

---

##  Built With

- SwiftUI
- iOS 17+
- No backend required (simulated data)
